01 — About

I'm a second-year Honours Physics student at McGill University, considering a joint Honours in Physics and Computer Science. I'm especially drawn to astrophysics and computational physics — using simulation and data to make sense of physical systems, from spacecraft in orbit to objects in the night sky.

Before McGill I studied at Aiglon College in the Swiss Alps, graduating with Honours and Distinction in the IB and a departmental award in Computer Science.

Outside of physics I ski, scuba dive, and shoot photography — from dark-sky astrophotography to everyday moments wherever I travel.
